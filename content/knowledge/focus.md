Right now Priyanshu works on two separate things:

- At his Sidecar internship, he builds a self-hosted support-ticket triage model. It is a fine-tuned classification
  model, not a RAG system.
- On his own, he is learning and building agentic RAG pipelines and applications, as part of his path to AI Engineer
  roles.

In agentic RAG, an LLM agent decides what to search for, calls retrieval and other tools, and checks the evidence before
it answers. His earlier projects came before this work and each covers one part of it: LexIQ covers retrieval and
evaluation, and Tandem covers multiple agents with tools.
