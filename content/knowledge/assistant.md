## How this chat works

<!-- keywords: bot, chatbot, assistant, you, this, chat, how, work, works, built, made, rag, retrieval, model, openai, gpt, chatgpt, groq, llm, ai -->

This chat is a small retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system that Priyanshu built into his website. His resume and
profile notes are split into short sections. For each question, a BM25 keyword search picks the most relevant sections,
and a large language model writes the answer from those sections only.
